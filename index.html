My Projects

Sentinel-2 Satellite Data Warehouse
Turns satellite images of the GTA into a table of vegetation per H3 cell per date, to find where the region lost green between summer 2022 and summer 2026. Python, PySpark, Delta Lake, dbt on DuckDB, Airflow 3, S3, and GitHub Actions. The answer so far was actually not what I expected... summer 2026 was greener overall, showing that the GTA has been getting more green over the past 5 years, which was surprising.
